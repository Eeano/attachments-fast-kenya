How Attachment Fast Kenya Works

For Students:
1. Fill Student Form above - Course + College + County + When
2. You get a Placement ID like AT-124 - save it
3. We try to find a company for you
4. Company tells you THEIR stipend / terms. You agree or you don't start.
5. Report PLACEMENT DONE / NO PLACEMENT with your Placement ID

For Companies:
1. Fill Company Form
2. You get a Placement ID
3. We try to find students who match your course
4. Student sends CV / Intro Letter via Attachment Fast Kenya
5. You interview + accept. Student starts. You sign logbook.
6. Pay fee AFTER student starts.

Trust & Safety

- Company / Student phone number is NEVER shown on the website. You connect via Attachment Fast Kenya.
- We do NOT show "Verified" unless we have checked it.
- Phone Checked = We called the number and spoke to the company / student.
- ⭐ Top Company = 3+ placements reported DONE, no complaints.
- New Listing = Just joined, phone check pending.
- 2x NO SHOW reports = Company / Student is removed.
- Always agree stipend, hours, duties and logbook signing BEFORE student starts.
- Do not pay any fee in advance to a company to get attachment. Report anyone asking for money to give you attachment.
